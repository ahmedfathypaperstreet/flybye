Support

Need help with FlyBye? Here are answers to the most common questions, and how to reach us if you don't find what you need.

Email: support@flybye.app

We typically respond within 24 hours, Monday–Friday.

Account

How do I create an account?

Tap "Create account" on the welcome screen, enter your email, password, and birthdate (you must be 18 or older), then complete onboarding by uploading a profile photo and a verification selfie.

I forgot my password.

From the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?". We'll email you a link to reset it. The link opens FlyBye directly — no need to copy and paste.

How do I delete my account?

Email support@flybye.app from the address tied to your account and we'll permanently delete it within 30 days. In a future update, you'll be able to delete your account directly in the app.

How do I change my email or password?

Open Settings → Account. To change your password, tap "Change password" and we'll send a reset link. Email changes currently require a quick request to support@flybye.app.

Verification

What is the verification selfie for?

To keep FlyBye real. Every member submits a selfie at signup, which we hold privately and review to make sure profiles are people, not bots or stolen photos. Your selfie is never shown to other users.

How long does verification take?

Usually a few hours during weekdays. You can use FlyBye while you wait — your profile will show a "Verification pending" badge until we approve it.

My verification was rejected.

You'll receive a notification with the reason. Common causes: blurry selfie, face not clearly visible, photo of a screen rather than a live capture. Re-take the selfie in good lighting and try again.

Discover & Matches

Why don't I see anyone in the discover deck?

  • You may not have added a flight yet. Tap the airplane icon in the Discover screen to add one.
  • Few or no other FlyBye users are at your airport right now. Try a major hub like JFK, LAX, or Heathrow.
  • If your departure time has passed, your flight is no longer "active" and your discover feed disappears. Add a new flight if you're at a different airport.

How do matches work?

If you and another user both like each other's profiles, you'll match instantly and a chat thread opens. Either of you can send the first message.

Can I unmatch someone?

Yes. Open the chat, tap the ⋯ menu in the top-right, and choose "Report and block." Blocking removes you from each other's discover feed and ends the chat.

Subscriptions

What's included in each tier?

  • Standby — Free. Swipe, match, and chat without limits.
  • Regular — Priority placement in the discover feed and unlimited matches.
  • First Class — Top placement at every airport, read receipts, and the First Class badge.

How do I cancel my subscription?

FlyBye subscriptions are billed through Apple. To cancel, open the iOS Settings app, tap your name → Subscriptions → FlyBye → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period.

I was charged but my subscription isn't active.

Open the Paywall in FlyBye and tap "Restore" in the top-right. If that doesn't resolve it, email support@flybye.app with your Apple receipt.

Safety

Someone is harassing me. What do I do?

Tap the ⋯ menu in any chat or profile and choose "Report and block." Reports go to our moderation queue and are reviewed promptly. For urgent safety issues, contact local authorities directly.

Someone has fake photos.

Use the same Report option. We will investigate and may remove the account. Please don't engage further — we'd rather hear about it than have you confront the user.

Tips for meeting in person at the airport

  • Meet only in busy, public terminal areas — food courts, gates with active traffic.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels off, walk away.
  • Tell a friend or family member where you'll be.
  • Don't share personal info like home address or financial details.
